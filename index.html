What Focusmith does

Consistency you can actually keep

Most habit apps count streaks, and a broken streak is where people quit. Focusmith scores the last 30 days instead — one missed day costs a few points, never everything.

📊

Consistency, not streaks

A rolling 30-day score that reflects how you're really doing. Miss a day and it dips a little. It never resets to zero.

⏸️

Pause days

Sick, travelling, or just need a rest? Mark a pause day and it leaves your score entirely — not counted as a miss.

🛡️

Fortress Mode

Focus sessions from 15 to 120 minutes with optional app blocking. It removes the temptation instead of asking you to resist it.

🔥

Heat map & insights

Six months of progress at a glance, consistency trends, focus hours and habit rankings.

📖

Learn the science

14 articles on habit formation, attention and self-regulation — each with its original scientific references.

🔐

Private by design

No account, no server, no tracking. Everything lives on your device.
