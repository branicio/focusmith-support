Privacy Policy

Focusmith — Habit Tracker & App Blocker

Effective November 9, 2026

Our commitment: Focusmith is built with your privacy as a priority. We do not collect, store, or share any personal information. All of your data stays on your device.

Information We Collect

Focusmith does not collect any personal information. Everything the app creates stays on your device:

  • Habit data: your habits, completion records and consistency scores are stored only on your device.
  • Focus session data: Fortress Mode session history and statistics remain on your device.
  • Preferences: your settings (appearance, notifications, language) are stored locally.

Screen Time

On iPhone and iPad, Focusmith uses Apple's Screen Time to block apps during Fortress Mode sessions. This works entirely on your device:

  • App selection and blocking preferences are managed locally by the operating system
  • No data about your app usage or blocked apps leaves your device
  • Screen Time permissions are handled by iOS itself

App blocking on Android

On Android, Fortress Mode blocks apps with two permissions you grant in Android’s settings: Usage access and “Display over other apps”. To let you choose what to block, Focusmith reads the list of your installed apps on your device. While a session runs, it uses usage access only to see which app is in the foreground, so that it can show its block screen over an app you chose to block. This check runs on your device: nothing about your app usage is collected or sent anywhere, and the list of apps you chose stays on your device.

Notifications

Focusmith may send local notifications to remind you about habits and focus sessions. These notifications are scheduled and delivered entirely on your device. They are not push notifications — no external server is involved — and they are fully controlled by your device's notification settings.

Third-Party Services

Focusmith uses no third-party services, external APIs, analytics tools, advertising SDKs or tracking technologies. We do not collect usage statistics, crash reports or behavioural data.

Data Storage

All data is stored locally on your device: habit records and completion history, focus session statistics, preferences and settings, and subscription status (managed by Apple or Google Play). We do not operate servers that store your personal data. There are no user accounts and no cloud sync.

Subscriptions

Focusmith offers a subscription, Focusmith Premium. On iPhone and iPad, all subscription management and payment processing is handled by Apple through the App Store. On Android, purchases go through Google Play’s billing system: Google processes the payment, and the app receives only information about the purchase itself (such as which plan you bought and whether it is active), never your payment details. We have no access to your payment information.

Children's Privacy

Focusmith does not knowingly collect any personal information from children. The app is rated 4+ and contains no objectionable content.

Your Rights

The app itself collects no personal data; for this website and for emails you send us, see “Controller and your rights (GDPR)” below. Your locally stored data can be removed by deleting the app.
